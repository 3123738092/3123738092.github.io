**Advisor:** Prof. Hui Xiong, AI Thrust, HKUST(GZ) · **Jun 2025 – Dec 2025** · **Accepted at ECCV 2026**

Built a physically-grounded event-camera noise simulator and validated realism through downstream tasks:

- Systematically collected scene datasets under **zero-lux, strong exposure, and high-contrast** conditions to analyze noise behavior.
- Implemented core EVS noise-model components: **Q-function event-probability estimation** and intensity-to-voltage domain mapping.
- Evaluated simulator realism by fine-tuning SOTA video interpolation & deblurring models (**TimeLens, EFNet**) on the synthetic data.
